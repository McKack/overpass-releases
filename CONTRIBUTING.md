# Contributing

Bug reports, test results and findings are very welcome. For a problem with a game or app, start the report
from OverPass with **Report Issue…**: it fills in your Mac, macOS and OverPass details and saves the log zip to
attach. Reports made that way are checked automatically and closed, with the reason, when the details were
changed or the zip is missing; editing the issue to fix it reopens it.

For anything else, open an issue with what you saw, how to reproduce it, and your Mac, macOS and OverPass
versions.

Pull requests must be written by people. We don't accept pull requests generated by AI agents or language
models, or opened in bulk. Using AI for your own research is fine: share what you found in an issue instead.

Keep pull requests small and about one thing. Say what was wrong and how you tested the fix, and follow the
existing code style.
